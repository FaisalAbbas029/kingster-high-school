Things To Know First

The Common Application is required for students applying to any or all of KUS three degree. You'll be able to choose your campus and program in that you are interested.

  • Contact information of the counselor or other school representative who will complete your Common Application School Report and submit your official high school transcript.
  • Contact information of one teacher for two maximum who will complete the Teacher Evaluation.
  • Nonrefundable $50 application fee. Students who are unable to pay the application fee can request a fee waiver.
